ABOUT

The silicon way: Si + Bahn

SIBAN LLC is a Russian supplier and integrator in electronics. We kit projects to BOM, supply components and equipment, and organise PCB/PCBA fabrication and contract manufacturing at specialised partner facilities. We handle import ourselves — you receive products from a Russian supplier.

What our mark means

  • Chip package — electronic components, the foundation of our work.
  • Silicon, element No. 14 — the material of all modern electronics.
  • The S-shaped trace — the company name and the supply route, laid out like a PCB track.
  • The circle — the world: suppliers, alternatives, foreign trade. The square — the finished product at the customer's site.

Principles

  • Project coordination at every stage — from request to delivery.
  • Transparent lead times and supply terms.
  • Incoming inspection and shipping documents.
